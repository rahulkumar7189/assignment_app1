Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you register on AcadMate, we collect the following information:

  • Full Name — to identify you on the platform
  • College Email — for account authentication
  • Phone Number — shared with the other party after contact unlock
  • WhatsApp Number (optional) — shared after contact unlock if provided
  • Telegram ID (optional) — shared after contact unlock if provided

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To match students with helpers for academic requests
  • To reveal contact details (phone, email, WhatsApp, Telegram) to the other party after the ₹20 contact-unlock fee is paid
  • To send in-platform notifications about your requests
  • To maintain platform security and prevent abuse

3. Payment Data

AcadMate does not store your card numbers, UPI IDs, or any payment credentials. All payment processing is handled securely by Razorpay. We only store a payment reference ID and the amount paid (₹20) to confirm contact unlock status.

4. Data Sharing

Your contact details are shared only with the specific student or helper on a request where the ₹20 unlock fee has been paid. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Cookies & Sessions

AcadMate uses session cookies only to keep you logged in. We do not use third-party tracking cookies or analytics cookies. You can clear cookies at any time through your browser settings.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Your account data is retained as long as your account is active. To request permanent deletion of your account and all associated data, contact the platform administrator at admin@cvru.ac.in. Deletion requests are processed within 7 business days.

7. Contact

For any privacy concerns or data requests, contact us at admin@cvru.ac.in.
