Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you register on AcadMate, we collect the following information:

  • Full Name — your first name is shown anonymously to the other party
  • College Email — for account authentication only, never shared
  • Phone Number — used for payment-related communication only, never shared with other users
  • UPI ID (helpers only) — used exclusively to transfer payouts after assignment completion

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To match students with helpers for academic requests
  • To process payments and transfer helper payouts via Razorpay
  • To send in-platform notifications about your requests
  • To maintain platform security and prevent abuse

Anonymity guarantee: Contact details (phone, email, WhatsApp, Telegram) of students and helpers are never shared with each other. All communication and work delivery happens exclusively through the AcadMate platform.

3. Payment Data

AcadMate does not store your card numbers, bank account details, or full UPI credentials. Helpers' UPI IDs are stored only for payout purposes. All payment processing is handled securely by Razorpay. We store only payment reference IDs, amounts, and split details for record-keeping and support.

4. Data Sharing

Your personal information is never shared with other users of the platform. Only a helper's first name is shown to students (and vice versa). We share your name, email, and UPI ID with Razorpay only as required to process payouts. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Cookies & Sessions

AcadMate uses session cookies only to keep you logged in. We do not use third-party tracking cookies or analytics cookies. You can clear cookies at any time through your browser settings.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Your account data is retained as long as your account is active. To request permanent deletion of your account and all associated data, contact the platform administrator at admin@cvru.ac.in. Deletion requests are processed within 7 business days.

7. Contact

For any privacy concerns or data requests, contact us at admin@cvru.ac.in.
