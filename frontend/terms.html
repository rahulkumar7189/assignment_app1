Terms & Conditions

1. Academic Integrity

AcadMate is a peer-to-peer academic support platform. Users agree to use the platform solely for guidance, clarification of concepts, and reference solutions. Users must not use AcadMate for direct assignment completion, exam cheating, or any form of academic misconduct.

2. How Payments Work

AcadMate uses a transparent anonymous escrow model:

  • Listing Fee (₹9): A non-refundable ₹9 fee is required to publish an assignment so helpers can find it.
  • Assignment Budget: The student sets a budget when posting. This amount is paid only after admin verifies the completed work.
  • Platform Commission: 10% of the assignment budget is retained by the platform.
  • Helper Payout: 90% of the budget is automatically transferred to the helper's registered UPI ID upon student payment.
  • Anonymity: Contact details of students and helpers are never revealed. All work is submitted and delivered through the platform only.

3. Limitation of Liability

Assignment Helper acts only as an anonymous assignment delivery platform. All work is submitted through the platform and verified by admin before payment. Any dispute, fraud, misconduct, plagiarism, or academic integrity violation arising from assignments is solely the responsibility of the parties involved. Assignment Helper shall not be liable for any damages.

4. User Conduct

Professional behavior is expected at all times. Helpers must not include personal contact information (phone, email, WhatsApp, social media) anywhere in submitted work files or chat messages. Doing so will result in immediate account suspension. Harassment, spam, or sharing of inappropriate content will lead to a permanent ban.

5. Refund Policy

The ₹9 listing fee is non-refundable in all cases. The assignment budget payment is non-refundable once the work file has been delivered to the student. If a verified technical error on our platform prevented delivery despite confirmed payment, please contact admin for review within 48 hours.
